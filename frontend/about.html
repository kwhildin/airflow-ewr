About AirFlow

Built for calmer travel days.

AirFlow helps travelers at EWR make smarter decisions with flight-aware leave times, security estimates, and terminal navigation.

Flight-aware timing

Leave-by and timeline steps follow your flight time, terminal, and the options you choose—so the plan stays tied to what you’re actually flying.

Security + walking estimates

Security wait context and walking time to the gate help you stress-test the plan before you leave for the airport.

Airport navigation support

Maps and terminal context make it easier to orient yourself once you’re on the ground at EWR.
